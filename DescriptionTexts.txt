1 (Fig. 1) - Pipeline diagram. A team repository is parsed by static analysers into business functions, call graphs and Mermaid diagrams, which feed LLM question generation. Students answer four questions; the four feedback configurations are randomly permuted across the answers; the evaluator receives a reference context from the code and produces Sandwich, Rubric, Explainable or Researching feedback, which students rate on 15 items.
