## Version Alignment Note (Collection Wording vs. Post-Collection Metadata Reconciliation)

The survey questions shown to students were **not** altered during or after data collection.
- **October 31 Metadata Header Reconciliation (`PACE_Tieu_Chi_Khao_Sat_2026-10-31.xlsx`, SHA-256 `d4e5da542489acf393e54fa989029c828be577d477e504aa094587890bcef16c` vs. Raw Export `PACE-BaiDaNop-DaDanhGiaAI-2026-10-29 (5).xlsx`, SHA-256 `aa57ae5d8458ad5d968a6ec727020fd169eaf9a322c20bd11f14278725e2a240`):** In the raw admin Excel export template, column 13 had retained a legacy metadata header string `Ý định từ chối(*) (REJECTION)` from an earlier draft template.
