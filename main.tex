\begin{abstract}
Oral defense (viva) in software engineering capstone projects is essential for verifying students' comprehension of business logic and codebase architecture, yet manual grading is labor-intensive and inconsistent, whereas off-the-shelf large language models (LLMs) risk code-context hallucinations, prompt injection, and sycophancy. This paper presents the pedagogical evaluation engine of \textbf{PACE}, an AI-assisted viva system integrating complexity-aware hybrid ground-truth extraction (routing raw code versus Mermaid control/data-flow graphs at node threshold $\tau = 10$) with three-layer guardrails and XML-based delimiter sandboxing. To mitigate single-model sycophancy, we propose a role-decoupled \textbf{Multi-LLM Ensemble} (\textit{Researching} mode) chaining \textbf{DeepSeek-V3} (\textit{Code Inspector}) with \textbf{Groq (\texttt{openai/gpt-oss-120b})} (\textit{Pedagogical Synthesizer and Rubric Grader}), evaluated alongside three single-LLM formative paradigms (\textit{Sandwich}, \textit{Rubric}, and \textit{Explainable}). In a randomized, blinded, within-subject experiment at FPT University, 86 students across 28 repositories submitted 344 scenario-based viva responses rated on a nine-dimension, five-point Likert instrument adapted from the Feedback Perceptions Questionnaire (FPQ) and formative assessment frameworks. Across 344 instances, ratings endorsed AI judgment (79.9\%), future use (68.0\%), and motivation (41.9\%), with a multidomain composite mean of 4.07/5 (95\% CI [3.90, 4.23]). All six 95\% repository-bootstrap contrasts on direct Acceptance included zero without establishing equivalence, and observational raw-versus-graph Acceptance remained inconclusive ($b = 0.03$, 95\% CI [$-0.18$, 0.26]). Exploratory \textit{Sandwich}--\textit{Explainable} actionability differed by 0.37 points (simultaneous 95\% sensitivity band [0.02, 0.72] across 90 contrasts) without isolating next-step effects. These findings concern reported perceptions rather than expert-verified correctness, observed revision, or learning gains.

\keywords{Automated Oral Assessment \and Multi-LLM Ensemble \and Formative Feedback \and Pedagogical AI Guardrails \and Software Engineering Education.}
\end{abstract}
